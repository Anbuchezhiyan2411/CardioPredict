Clinical Workflow

Rigorous 4-Stage Diagnostic Pipeline

How CardioPredict ingests, standardizes, and evaluates multi-modal cardiovascular parameters in real time.

1. Intake & Schema

Ingests 13 vital signs across demographics, metabolic panels, treadmill stress ECGs, and nuclear thallium scintigraphy.

2. Standardization

Applies training-locked StandardScaler normalization to prevent features with large units (Cholesterol) from biasing the model.

3. Ensemble Inference

100 de-correlated Random Forest decision trees evaluate non-linear multi-variable interactions simultaneously without data leakage.

4. Risk Stratification

Outputs calibrated confidence percentages, classifies into Low, Moderate, or High risk tiers, and provides targeted clinical recommendations.
